## Goal
BETADIST_BETAINV G5, where Excel's BETA.DIST is correctly rounded and ours is visibly off, comes within 15 significant
digits of Excel through a more accurate incomplete beta at that point, and H5 (BETA.INV of G5) follows.

## Context
Gap triage 2026-10-31e (`make eval` on main 8a14b8b). These are two of the ironcalc-calc visibly-off cells behind M9's
unmet exit row (48 visibly off against an allowance of 36). `scripts/exit_owners.py --all-off` shows them owned only by
`defer: phase-1b` tasks (T-3374 puts G5 out of its scope; T-0970 and T-2119 left G5 as "160 ulp, Excel correctly
rounded"), so no live task owns them.
- ironcalc-calc/STATISTICAL/BETADIST_BETAINV.xlsx (2): Sheet1!G5, H5
- G5 `BETA.DIST(A5,B5,C5,D5,E5,F5)` = `BETA.DIST(33587,3,600,TRUE,2000,5489582)`: Excel 0.6734553797493481, ours
  0.6734553797493648 (`NumericClose`, 2.5E-14 relative). mpmath at 50 digits with x = 31587/5487582:
  0.67345537974934809577..., which rounds to Excel's value. So this is the `accuracy` stage: Excel is close to correctly
  rounded and the engine is not. The cell is an Excel-for-Mac cache (app 16.0300).
- H5 `BETA.INV(G5,3,600,2000,5489582)`: Excel 33587.000000000226, ours 33586.99999999992. BETA.INV solves our own cdf
  to the crossing (statistics.md "BETA.INV solves Excel's own CDF to the crossing (T-1745)"), so H5 is expected to
  follow once G5's cdf is right. Re-measure it after the G5 fix before touching the inverse.
- a = 3, b = 600, x about 0.0058: in TOMS 708 bratio this is the a0 > 1, b0 >= 40 region, served by `bfrac`
  (bratio/bfrac.rs, "Excel's beta density times x·y as its prefactor") or by the T-0970 kernel where bfrac hands the
  point back. Find which path G5 takes and which part carries the 160 ulp (the prefactor at b = 600, the continued
  fraction, or the [A, B] rescaling of x) before changing anything.
- Related: T-3322 (basym port), T-3374 (last bits of the same workbook), T-3392 (density). Name any of their cells
  this moves in the Outcome.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] G5 and H5 are within half a unit of the 15th significant digit of Excel's value (`rustsheet-eval show`). The G5
      line leaves `eval/baseline/ironcalc-calc.txt`, and so does the H5 line unless the Outcome shows that what is
      left is the inverse's own residue. No new known-failure line appears in any baseline.
- [ ] Gate A envelope on a fresh sweep (decision 0188 R2): a new probes-numeric case, filled by the oracle, of BETA.DIST
      cumulative with a from 1 to 10 and b from 40 to 5000 around x = a/(a+b), scored against a 50-digit mpmath
      reference. The Outcome reports, per stratum, the points within 15 digits of Excel and within 1E-9 relative of
      the math: 0 points outside both, and 0 cells where one side is an error and the other a number.
- [ ] The fix is a more accurate kernel, not a fit to Excel's bits (the `accuracy` stage); statistics.md gets an
      "Ours" line naming the change.
- [ ] `make check` green; reviewers approve.
